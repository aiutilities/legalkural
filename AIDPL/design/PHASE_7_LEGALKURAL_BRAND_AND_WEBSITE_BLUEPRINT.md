# Phase 7 — LegalKural Brand and Website Blueprint

## Status

`APPROVED — FOUNDER APPROVED`

The Founder approved this blueprint on 2026-08-19. B4 offline journal visual
implementation and B5 offline accessibility, SEO, privacy and legal-page
specification are authorized. WordPress access, provider requests, publication,
Coming Soon removal and public launch remain unauthorized.

## Founder-Selected Identity

- Canonical brand: `LegalKural`
- Tagline: `The court records events. We reveal their meaning.`
- Direction: contemporary legal-tech
- Core palette: navy, teal and white

Use `LegalKural` as one word in product-facing identity. Existing technical or
historical references to “Legal Kural” need not be mechanically rewritten.

## Brand Position

LegalKural is an AI-native legal knowledge and interpretation platform that
turns judgments into structured, source-grounded and readable legal insight.

It is not a law firm, legal-advice service, court, government service or
substitute for the source judgment.

## Brand Promise

LegalKural preserves the authority of the court record while helping readers
understand the events, issues, reasoning, decision and wider legal principle.

The visual identity should feel:

- authoritative, not ceremonial;
- intelligent, not futuristic or gimmicky;
- clear, not simplified beyond legal accuracy;
- contemporary, not corporate-generic;
- distinctly Indian in context without decorative cultural imitation.

## Logo Direction

### Recommended mark

A compact `LK` monogram constructed from two interlocking vertical forms:

- the `L` establishes a stable legal foundation;
- the diagonal of the `K` opens forward like a page or interpretive path;
- a restrained teal line separates record from meaning;
- geometry remains legible at favicon size.

The mark must not use scales of justice, gavels, courthouse columns, AI brains,
robot imagery or ornamental Thirukkural/Tamil motifs.

### Wordmark

Use `Legal` in navy and `Kural` in teal, with equal typographic weight. Avoid
camel-case exaggeration, gradients, shadows and narrow futuristic lettering.

### Lockups

Required offline assets:

- horizontal mark + wordmark;
- stacked mark + wordmark + tagline;
- symbol-only favicon;
- single-colour navy version;
- reversed white version for navy backgrounds.

## Typography

### Website

- Headings: `Source Serif 4`, with Georgia as fallback.
- Body/interface: `Inter`, with system sans-serif fallback.
- Legal citations/data: body family with tabular numerals where available.

If WordPress.com cannot load these fonts safely under the selected plan, use:

- headings: Georgia, serif;
- body: system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif.

### Journal

Retain embedded deterministic fonts. Do not introduce network fonts. The
renderer may adopt the brand hierarchy through size, spacing, colour and rules
while keeping bundled font files and reproducibility.

## Voice and Editorial Style

Use clear Indian English with restrained authority.

- Lead with meaning, then evidence.
- Prefer precise sentences over slogans.
- Distinguish fact, inference and interpretation.
- Never imply that original LegalKural writing is an authentic Thirukkural.
- Never imply legal advice or a court/government affiliation.
- Keep the selected tagline unchanged in the primary brand lockup.

## Homepage Blueprint

### 1. Header

- horizontal LegalKural logo;
- primary navigation;
- search affordance;
- no oversized promotional button.

### 2. Hero

Heading:

`The court records events. We reveal their meaning.`

Supporting copy:

`Source-grounded explanations of judgments, legal reasoning and the principles
that shape everyday life.`

Primary action: `Explore judgments`

Secondary action: `About LegalKural`

### 3. Latest legal insight

Feature the latest certified article with title, court, date, category, concise
excerpt and a restrained `Read analysis` link.

### 4. How LegalKural reads a judgment

Four steps:

1. Preserve the source.
2. Structure the case.
3. Trace the reasoning.
4. Explain the principle.

### 5. Explore by legal area

Use verified categories only. Do not create empty or speculative taxonomy.

### 6. Journal

Introduce the verified weekly journal only when a production edition exists.
Until then, omit this section rather than displaying Coming Soon filler.

### 7. Trust statement

State that every analysis links back to its source record and passes governed
legal, editorial and QA review. Clarify that content is informational and not
legal advice.

### 8. Footer

Logo, one-sentence description, utility links, copyright, privacy and legal
disclaimer. Remove all WordPress/theme placeholder credits where the plan and
theme permit this lawfully.

## Article Page Blueprint

Preserve certified article content and ordering. Presentation may add:

- breadcrumb;
- category, court and decision date;
- clear article title and excerpt;
- reading-width container of approximately 680–760 px;
- accessible heading hierarchy;
- styled LegalKural principle/verse panel;
- source-judgment link or approved document access;
- disclaimer and related verified judgments;
- sharing metadata without intrusive widgets.

Post ID `10` must not be rewritten merely to apply a theme. Its certified
content hash remains protected unless a separately approved content migration
is required.

## Required Page Blueprint

### About

Explain purpose, governed process, source grounding, editorial boundaries and
the relationship between LegalKural, ThinkingOS and AIDPL in reader-friendly
language.

### Contact

Provide an approved production contact channel. Do not expose personal contact
details without explicit Founder approval.

### Privacy

Describe only actual data collection. Do not claim analytics, cookies,
subscriptions or forms that are not enabled. WordPress.com platform behavior
must be verified before final wording.

### Disclaimer

State that content is informational, not legal advice; source judgments govern;
court/government affiliation is not implied; and original LegalKural lines are
not authentic Thirukkural verses.

## Journal Visual Blueprint

### Cover

- navy masthead band;
- LegalKural wordmark and selected tagline;
- journal title, edition date and journal ID;
- thin teal rule;
- white/mist field with ample space;
- no decorative stock imagery.

### Contents

- clear article order and page numbers;
- court/category metadata as secondary text;
- strong alignment and generous spacing.

### Article opening

- article number and title;
- court, decision date and category;
- subtle teal rule;
- readable single-column body;
- preserved source and disclaimer information.

### Page furniture

- `LegalKural` at left or centre;
- edition/journal ID where space permits;
- page number;
- no footer collision with content.

### Deterministic boundary

Final dressing must retain:

- A4 print-safe margins;
- embedded fonts;
- deterministic metadata and byte output;
- English-only journal body;
- Tamil rendering disabled;
- `TITLE_ONLY` Kural algorithm usage;
- existing manifest, verification and archive contracts.

## Accessibility Baseline

- WCAG-oriented AA contrast for normal text and controls;
- visible keyboard focus;
- logical heading sequence;
- 16 px minimum website body size;
- 44 px preferred touch targets;
- descriptive links and image alternative text;
- no colour-only status communication;
- responsive layouts at narrow mobile widths;
- reduced-motion compatibility if motion is ever introduced.

## SEO and Sharing Baseline

- unique title and meta description per page;
- canonical URLs;
- Open Graph title, description and image;
- descriptive article slugs;
- verified sitemap/indexing settings;
- Organization and Article structured data only when factually supported;
- no invented author credentials or publication dates.

## WordPress.com Implementation Boundary

Before live configuration, B6 must verify:

- current plan and theme capabilities;
- Site Editor/custom CSS/font availability;
- page, menu, widget and template state;
- site title/tagline and favicon state;
- Coming Soon/launch controls;
- backup/export and rollback options;
- platform-injected cookie, privacy and footer behavior.

No assumption in this blueprint overrides an observed WordPress.com limitation.
