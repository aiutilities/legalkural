## Methodology

H1: `How LegalKural works`

### Source first

Every explanation begins with an identifiable source judgment. The source and
official record govern if any difference exists.

### Structured review

LegalKural separates facts, issues, reasoning, holdings and practical meaning.
Legal fidelity and quality checks precede publication.

### Human approval

Publication remains a deliberate editorial action. Automated assistance does
not replace Founder authorization or professional judgment.

### Transparent limits

LegalKural explanations are general information, not legal advice. Law changes,
later decisions may affect an earlier explanation, and specific situations
require advice from a qualified professional.
