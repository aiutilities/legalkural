## Ownership Map

| Artifact | Owning Agent | Reviewer |
|---|---|---|
| Manifest and integrity | Intake | QA |
| Metadata to evidence | Extraction | QA |
| Law | Legal Analysis | QA |
| Reasoning and decision | Judicial Reasoning | QA |
| Kural | Kural Reasoning | QA |
| Article | Legal Editorial | QA |
| Thinking review | LearningOS | AI CEO |

## Non-Negotiable Rule

No agent approves its own output.
