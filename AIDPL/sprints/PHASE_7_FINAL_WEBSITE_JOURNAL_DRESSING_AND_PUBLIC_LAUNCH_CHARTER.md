## Roadmap Alignment

Phase 7 is the final governed roadmap phase:

- final website and journal dressing;
- production WordPress theme and navigation;
- final journal visual design;
- accessibility, SEO and privacy review;
- controlled public launch;
- final product handover.

## Objective

Turn the certified LegalKural publication and journal capabilities into a
coherent, accessible and launch-ready public product without weakening any
legal, editorial, publication, archive or production-operations gate.

## Discovery Finding

The repository contains certified WordPress publishing integrations and a
repository-owned journal renderer, but it does not contain a repository-owned
WordPress theme or website implementation.

Phase 7 therefore has two separately governed workstreams:

1. offline, repository-controlled journal and presentation development;
2. explicitly authorized WordPress.com site configuration and visual changes.

WordPress.com changes must be planned and evidenced separately. Repository
tests cannot by themselves certify live site appearance.

## Core Principles

1. Design work must not alter certified legal or editorial substance.
2. Offline preparation precedes every live WordPress action.
3. Read-only inspection, draft/configuration changes and public launch are
   separate authorization gates.
4. Coming Soon removal is the final launch action, never an incidental theme
   or navigation change.
5. Every live mutation must have before/after evidence and rollback guidance.
6. No credential, OAuth file, token or live response is committed.

## Workstream A — Brand and Website Blueprint

Define and obtain Founder approval for:

- product name presentation;
- site title and tagline;
- logo and visual identity;
- colour and typography system;
- homepage information architecture;
- header, footer and navigation;
- article, category, archive and search presentation;
- About, Contact, Privacy and Disclaimer pages;
- removal of Hello World and placeholder content;
- responsive mobile and desktop behavior;
- production contact information.

Brand copy and legal-policy copy require explicit review before live use.

## Workstream B — WordPress.com Production Dressing

After the blueprint is approved:

- inspect current WordPress.com plan and supported theme/editor capabilities;
- inventory current pages, posts, menus, widgets, styles and Coming Soon state;
- prepare an exact mutation and rollback plan;
- configure the approved production theme and styles;
- build homepage, navigation, footer and required pages;
- preserve published post ID `10` and its certified content;
- remove default and placeholder content only after verification;
- verify anonymous, authenticated, desktop and mobile presentation.

No live WordPress action is authorized merely by approval of this charter.
Each live block requires its own Founder authorization.

## Workstream C — Final Journal Visual Design

Improve the repository-owned journal renderer while preserving immutable
edition and verification contracts:

- cover and masthead;
- hierarchy, spacing and typography;
- contents and article-opening presentation;
- page furniture, numbering and footers;
- legal-source and disclaimer presentation;
- accessible contrast and readable type sizes;
- deterministic rendering and checksums;
- print and screen review.

Journal body remains English-only. Tamil rendering remains disabled. The
Thirukkural-inspired algorithm remains restricted to `TITLE_ONLY`.

## Workstream D — Accessibility, SEO and Privacy

Certify:

- keyboard and focus behavior where applicable;
- headings, landmarks and meaningful link text;
- colour contrast and responsive readability;
- image alternative text;
- page titles and descriptions;
- canonical URL and sharing metadata;
- sitemap and indexing configuration;
- Privacy, Disclaimer and Contact content;
- no credential or personal-data leakage;
- no analytics, cookies or subscriptions unless separately approved.

## Workstream E — Controlled Public Launch

Launch requires a separate final Founder decision after all other gates pass.

The launch sequence must include:

1. certified backup and rollback checkpoint;
2. pre-launch anonymous and authenticated review;
3. content, navigation, accessibility, SEO and privacy sign-off;
4. explicit Founder launch authorization;
5. deliberate Coming Soon removal;
6. anonymous production verification;
7. evidence capture and incident rollback readiness;
8. final handover and remote repository protection.

## Explicit Non-Goals

Phase 7 does not authorize:

- changes to legal reasoning or certified article substance;
- republishing or replacing post ID `10` without a separate content gate;
- automatic publication of additional articles;
- provider/model requests for design work without approval;
- subscriptions, payments, advertising or analytics by default;
- deterministic Tamil generation or Tamil journal rendering;
- deleting production evidence, backups or archives;
- bypassing WordPress.com plan or platform restrictions;
- a custom WordPress theme when the selected plan cannot safely support it.
