# 1. Purpose

Legal Kural exists to transform court judgments into accurate, understandable and enduring public knowledge.

Legal Kural shall help:

- Citizens understand justice.
- Students understand legal reasoning.
- Lawyers identify reusable legal patterns.
- Researchers trace judicial principles.
- Society learn from decided cases.

Legal Kural is not merely a legal blog.

Legal Kural is an AI-native Legal Knowledge and Interpretation Platform.

---

# 2. Product Mission

Transform every suitable court judgment into:

- A faithful case record.

- A structured legal study.
- A memorable Kural-inspired insight.

# 8. Editorial Laws

## Law 1 — Facts Are Sacred

No fact may be invented, assumed or silently altered.

## Law 2 — Sources Are Traceable

Every material factual claim must be traceable to the source judgment or an explicitly identified external authority.

## Law 3 — Fact and Interpretation Are Separate

The platform shall clearly distinguish:

- What the judgment states.
- What the court concludes.
- What Legal Kural interprets.
- What remains uncertain.

## Law 4 — Chronology Precedes Commentary

The case timeline shall be reconstructed before moral, legal or editorial interpretation.

## Law 5 — Neutrality Precedes Drama

Storytelling may improve understanding.

Storytelling shall never distort evidence, parties, judicial reasoning or outcome.

## Law 6 — Clarity Shall Not Become Oversimplification

Legal complexity may be reduced.

Legal meaning shall not be removed.

## Law 7 — Uncertainty Must Be Visible

Where the record is incomplete, ambiguous or conflicting, the output shall say so.

## Law 8 — No Legal Advice

Legal Kural may explain law and judgments.

It shall not present general content as personalised legal advice.

## Law 9 — Respect for Persons

The platform shall avoid unnecessary humiliation, sensationalism and dehumanising language.

## Law 10 — Corrections Are Additive

Published errors shall be corrected transparently.

Historical versions shall not be silently rewritten.

# 9. Kural Reasoning Principle

The Kural-inspired method is the distinctive reasoning layer of Legal Kural.

It shall:

1. Observe the complete factual record.
2. Identify the human conflict.
3. Identify the legal question.
4. Identify the governing principle.
5. Compress the principle into a memorable insight.
6. Expand the insight into faithful explanation.
7. Preserve the reasoning for future reuse.

The Kural-inspired insight shall not be falsely presented as an authentic verse from the Thirukkural.

Any original AI-generated couplet, aphorism or insight shall be clearly identified as original Legal Kural editorial content.

Nothing may be published unless:

```
Source Verified       ✓
Facts Validated       ✓
Timeline Validated    ✓
Reasoning Traceable   ✓
Editorial Review      ✓
Legal Disclaimer      ✓
Founder Authorization ✓
Learning Captured     ✓
```

Draft generation is not publication authorization.

Technical readiness is not editorial authorization.

Founder authorization is required for public release until the Constitution is deliberately amended.

# 14. Data and Privacy

Legal Kural shall distinguish between:

- Public judgments.
- Public legal materials.
- Licensed materials.
- Confidential documents.
- Personal information.
- Restricted records.

Confidential or restricted material shall not be published merely because it was provided to the system.

Sensitive personal information shall be handled according to applicable law, source restrictions and editorial necessity.

---

# 15. Corrections and Disputes

Every published case shall support a correction process.

A correction request shall be evaluated using:

- Source judgment.
- Publication record.
- Version history.
- Supporting evidence.
- Editorial review.

Corrections shall record:

- What was incorrect.
- What was changed.
- Why it was changed.
- When it was changed.
- Who authorized the correction.

Legal Kural succeeds when:

- Readers understand judgments more clearly.
- Factual error rates decrease.

Traffic alone is not success.

# 17. Product Boundaries

Legal Kural shall not become:

- An unverified case-summary generator.
- A sensational legal-news portal.
- A substitute for professional legal advice.
- A source of fabricated quotations.
- A system that hides uncertainty.
- A publishing engine that prioritizes speed over truth.
- A platform that copies the Thirukkural without attribution or respect.
