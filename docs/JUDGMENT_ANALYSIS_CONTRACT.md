# Judgment Analysis Contract

**Document:** LK-CONTRACT-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Frozen

---

# Purpose

This contract defines the mandatory outputs for every judgment processed by Legal Kural.

No AI agent may skip or invent any section.

---

# Stage 1 — Document Verification

Required Outputs

- Source File Name
- Court
- Case Number
- Decision Date
- Language
- Number of Pages
- Document Completeness
- OCR Confidence (if applicable)

---

# Stage 2 — Metadata

Required Outputs

- Parties
- Judge(s)
- Bench
- Advocates
- Court Level
- Citation
- Jurisdiction

---

# Stage 3 — Timeline

Required Outputs

- Cause of Action
- Important Events
- Filing Date
- Hearings
- Orders
- Final Judgment

Timeline shall remain chronological.

---

# Stage 4 — Facts

Required Outputs

- Material Facts
- Undisputed Facts
- Disputed Facts

Facts shall never contain interpretation.

---

# Stage 5 — Issues

Required Outputs

- Legal Issues
- Factual Issues
- Procedural Issues

---

# Stage 6 — Evidence

Required Outputs

- Oral Evidence
- Documentary Evidence
- Electronic Evidence
- Court Findings on Evidence

Every evidence item shall reference its source.

---

# Stage 7 — Applicable Law

Required Outputs

- Statutes
- Rules
- Regulations
- Constitutional Provisions
- Case Law
- Legal Principles

---

# Stage 8 — Judicial Reasoning

Required Outputs

- Questions considered
- Court analysis
- Principles applied
- Reasons accepted
- Reasons rejected

Reasoning shall remain faithful to the judgment.

---

# Stage 9 — Decision

Required Outputs

- Final Order
- Relief Granted
- Relief Denied
- Costs
- Directions

---

# Stage 10 — ThinkingOS

Required Outputs

Attention

What deserves attention?

Observation

What are the facts?

Understanding

What is actually happening?

Reasoning

What principle explains this case?

Communication

Explain for:

- Citizen
- Student
- Lawyer

---

# Stage 11 — Kural Reasoning

Required Outputs

- Human Conflict
- Universal Principle
- Kural-inspired Insight
- Expanded Explanation

The Kural-inspired insight must be original editorial writing.

It shall never be presented as an authentic Thirukkural verse unless explicitly quoted and referenced.

---

# Stage 12 — LearningOS

Required Outputs

- New Story
- New Lesson
- New Pattern
- Improvement Opportunity

Every processed judgment improves the institution.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Every completed judgment shall satisfy:

[ ] Source Verified

[ ] Metadata Complete

[ ] Timeline Verified

[ ] Facts Verified

[ ] Issues Identified

[ ] Evidence Mapped

[ ] Law Identified

[ ] Judicial Reasoning Captured

[ ] Decision Captured

[ ] ThinkingOS Completed

[ ] Kural Reasoning Completed

[ ] LearningOS Updated

No judgment is complete until every item has been satisfied.
