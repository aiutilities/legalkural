# 001 — LegalKural Vision

## Status

`CANONICAL — NORMATIVE`

## Purpose

LegalKural turns verified Indian judgments into clear, source-grounded legal
explanations without changing the Court's facts, reasoning, holding, order or
limits.

The product promise is:

> The court records events. We reveal their meaning.

LegalKural exists to help ordinary readers, legal professionals, researchers
and institutions understand what a judgment decided, why it decided it and
where its application stops.

## Product Principles

1. **Source before narrative.** Every substantive claim must be traceable to
   the supplied judgment or an approved source artifact.
2. **Meaning without distortion.** Explanation may simplify language but must
   preserve legal effect, factual limits and procedural posture.
3. **Reasoning before reaction.** LegalKural explains the chain from material
   facts through issues, law and reasoning to the final decision.
4. **Evidence before automation.** Generated outputs require deterministic
   lineage, validation and human approval before publication.
5. **Clarity without legal advice.** Public material is general explanatory
   information and must not be presented as personalised legal advice.
6. **Governed originality.** The Thirukkural-inspired mechanism may create an
   original English editorial title only. It must never present generated text
   as an authentic Thirukkural verse.

## Language and Kural Boundary

- Public editorial output is English-only.
- Tamil rendering is disabled.
- Thirukkural-inspired algorithm usage is `TITLE_ONLY`.
- The algorithm must not generate or insert Tamil text, a couplet, a purported
  translation, an epigraph or body content.

## Product System

LegalKural is a governed pipeline, not a free-form blog generator. It preserves
an auditable flow from judgment ingestion through structured analysis,
editorial review, title generation, article assembly, WordPress preparation,
human approval, publication evidence, journal generation and archive.

## Public Experience

The website and journal must feel contemporary, credible and calm. Visual
design must support reading and trust; it must never sensationalise litigation
or imply authority beyond the judgment.

## Success Criteria

LegalKural is on track only when:

- the legal explanation is faithful and page-referenced;
- the reasoning chain and decision limits remain intact;
- each output has verifiable source and publication lineage;
- publication is deliberate, reversible and evidence-backed;
- accessibility, privacy and editorial integrity are preserved; and
- public launch occurs only after an explicit Founder-authorised gate.
