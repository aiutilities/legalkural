# Cogzidel Constitution (OSS)

**Document:** OSS-000
**Version:** 1.0 (Founder's Edition)
**Status:** Frozen

---

# Preamble

Cogzidel exists to discover, systemize, preserve and continuously improve knowledge that enables people and institutions to think better, build better and operate better.

Software is an expression of knowledge.

Knowledge is the enduring asset.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Constitution governs the institution.

Every Operating System, Product, Process and Decision derives its authority from this Constitution.

Nothing supersedes the Constitution.

---

# Constitutional Identity

Cogzidel is a Knowledge Systems Institution.

Not a software company.

Not an AI company.

Artificial Intelligence is a capability.

Knowledge is the foundation.

---

# Constitutional Hierarchy

```
Cogzidel Constitution
        │
        ├── ThinkingOS
        ├── AIDPL
        ├── LearningOS
        └── Products
```

Every Operating System derives its authority from the Constitution.

Every Product derives its authority from the Operating Systems.

# The Three Operating Systems

## ThinkingOS

Purpose

How the institution thinks.

Mission

Transform information into trustworthy knowledge.

Owns

- Attention
- Observation
- Understanding
- Reasoning
- Communication

Never owns execution.

---

## AIDPL

Purpose

How the institution builds.

Mission

Transform vision into repeatable execution.

Owns

- Planning
- Architecture
- Execution
- Validation
- Delivery

Never owns institutional philosophy.

---

## LearningOS

Purpose

How the institution learns.

Mission

Ensure valuable knowledge is never lost.

Owns

- Stories
- Lessons
- Patterns
- Standards
- Institutional Memory

Never owns execution.

---

# Founder

The Founder is the Steward of the Institution.

The Founder owns:

- Vision
- Philosophy
- Attention
- Authorization

The Founder does not own:

- Sprint execution
- Architecture decisions
- Product implementation
- Quality validation
- Institutional Memory

The Founder protects the Constitution.

The Founder does not bypass the Constitution.

---

# AI CEO

The AI CEO is the Institutional Executive.

Responsibilities

- Interpret Founder intent
- Coordinate ThinkingOS
- Govern AIDPL
- Coordinate AI Departments
- Protect quality
- Protect architecture
- Report institutional progress

The AI CEO owns execution.

The AI CEO never changes philosophy.

# Institutional Memory

Institutional Memory belongs to the Institution.

Never to individuals.

Institutional Memory permanently preserves:

- Stories
- Lessons
- Patterns
- Standards
- Decisions
- Evidence
- Architecture
- Questions
- Historical Context

Institutional Memory is additive.

History is never intentionally rewritten.
