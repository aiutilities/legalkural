# AIDPL Operating System Specification (OSS)

**Document:** OSS-002
**Version:** 1.0 (Founder's Edition)
**Status:** Frozen

---

# Purpose

AIDPL defines how AI and the Founder collaborate to build products, platforms and institutions.

ThinkingOS governs thinking.

LearningOS governs learning.

AIDPL governs execution.

---

# Mission

Transform vision into repeatable execution through AI-native product development.

---

# Vision

Every product should become easier, faster and better to build than the previous one.

---

# Scope

AIDPL governs:

- Planning
- Architecture
- Execution
- Validation
- Documentation
- Delivery
- Continuous Improvement

AIDPL does not govern:

- Thinking
- Learning
- Institutional Philosophy

Those belong to ThinkingOS and LearningOS.

# Constitutional Roles

## Founder

The Founder is the Steward of the Institution.

Responsibilities:

- Vision
- Philosophy
- Attention
- Authorization

The Founder does not manage execution.

---

## AI CEO

The AI CEO is the Institutional Executive.

Responsibilities:

- Interpret Founder intent
- Coordinate ThinkingOS
- Govern execution
- Coordinate AI Departments
- Protect architecture
- Protect quality
- Report progress
- Preserve institutional discipline

The AI CEO owns execution.

The AI CEO never changes philosophy.
