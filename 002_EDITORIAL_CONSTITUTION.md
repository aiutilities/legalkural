# 002 — Editorial Constitution

## Status

`CANONICAL — NORMATIVE`

## Editorial Duty

LegalKural owes its first duty to the supplied judgment. Readability,
originality, search visibility and visual presentation are subordinate to
legal fidelity.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

1. Do not invent a fact, date, party, submission, statute, precedent, finding,
   remedy or quotation.
2. Do not convert a party's allegation or argument into a Court finding.
3. Do not convert background discussion, obiter or an interim observation into
   the operative holding.
4. Preserve qualifications, exceptions, verification requirements,
   case-specific limits and the exact disposition.
5. Attribute propositions to the correct speaker: petitioner, respondent,
   authority, witness or Court.
6. Use page references for substantive legal, factual and procedural claims.
7. If the source does not support a claim, omit it or mark the gap for human
   review. Never fill the gap by inference.
8. Paraphrase responsibly. Use quotation marks only for verified quotations.
9. Distinguish explanation from personalised advice and display the approved
   legal-information disclaimer.
10. A model output is a proposal until required review and approval gates pass.

## Required Editorial Structure

An article must make the following understandable where the source supports
them:

- Court, judge, case identity and procedural posture;
- material facts and the dispute;
- issues the Court had to decide;
- parties' relevant positions without misattribution;
- governing law and authorities;
- the Court's reasoning chain;
- holding, operative directions and disposition;
- practical meaning and explicit limits; and
- source and editorial disclaimer.

Headings may be reader-friendly, but they must not change the legal hierarchy
defined by `004_JUDGMENT_GRAMMAR.md`.

## Title Constitution

The title must be an original English editorial title that captures the
verified legal principle without claiming to be a quotation or authentic
Thirukkural. The governed algorithm is defined in
`003_THIRUKKURAL_ALGORITHM.md` and is restricted to `TITLE_ONLY`.

## Human Gates

Publication requires, at minimum:

- legal-fidelity review;
- editorial-quality review;
- confirmation of judgment and artifact lineage;
- approval of title, article, taxonomy and author;
- preview of the exact WordPress payload; and
- explicit authorization for the draft or publication action being taken.

No approval for a draft, taxonomy or prior article is reusable as approval for
a different public publication action.

## Corrections

Material corrections must preserve the previous evidence, record what changed,
identify the approver and re-run the relevant verification gates. Silent
replacement of certified evidence is prohibited.

## Language Contract

Editorial output is English-only. Tamil rendering is disabled. No Tamil verse,
transliteration or purported translation may be added by the automated
pipeline.
